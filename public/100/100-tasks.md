# The 100 — Foodlog

> **Foodlog** · foodlog.xyz
>
> Foodlog is a desktop-first food, nutrition, meal-planning, exercise and weight-tracking dashboard — log meals, plan your week, and see FDA-style nutrition reports backed by USDA data. Here is every one of the 100 startup tasks, answered for Foodlog, with our status and next move.

**Progress snapshot:** ✅ 20 done · 🟡 53 in progress · ⬜ 27 to do (of 100).

Framework: *The 100 Tasks Startup System* by Martin Bell — Setup (1–25) · Launch (26–69) · Scale (70–100). The live, interactive checklist is at **https://foodlog.xyz/100**.
