Google Play Data Safety & Privacy

Delete your Foodlog account & data

You can permanently delete your Foodlog account (`xyz.foodlog.app` and `foodlog.xyz`) and all associated data either directly inside the Android app (Account → Delete Account & Data) or using the form below.

What gets permanently deleted immediately:

  • Your Foodlog user profile (name, email, avatar, Google account link, and password hash)
  • All nutrition diary entries, logged meals, meal photos, water intake, weight history, and custom foods
  • All active login sessions, OAuth tokens, and registered push notification device tokens
  • Any remaining AI photo scan credits and scan pass balances
